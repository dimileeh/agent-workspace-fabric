## Decision encoded (owner ruling)

The post-agent pre-commit repair plan-only check must judge "plan-only" from the
branch's **net committed-plus-staged** output, not the latest staged delta alone.
Gating `_fail_if_plan_only_paths` behind the #427 helper
`_committed_and_staged_output_is_plan_only(worktree_path, base_commit, repair_staged_paths)`
makes the genuine failure (R6: nothing real committed) still fire while removing the
false positive (R5: real implementation self-committed earlier, plan-only restage).
`PLAN_ONLY_OUTPUT_REASON_CODE` and `FailureReason.agent_failure` are unchanged, and no
other plan-only site (`execution_validation.py` #427, `execution_flow.py` #431/#432)
was touched.
